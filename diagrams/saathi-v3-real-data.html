Form 80 — Question 4 of 127

Date of Birth

Enter your date of birth in DD/MM/YYYY format as shown on your passport. The Department of Home Affairs cross-checks this against your passport data.

📖 What this means

Use the Australian date format (day/month/year). Do not use the US format (month/day/year).

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Using MM/DD/YYYY format (US — AU uses DD/MM/YYYY)
  • Date differs from passport — DHA cross-checks this
