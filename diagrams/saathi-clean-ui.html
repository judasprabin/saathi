Form 80 — Question 4 of 127

Date of Birth

Enter your date of birth in DD/MM/YYYY format as shown on your passport or birth certificate. The Department of Home Affairs cross-checks this field against your passport data.

What this means

This is your date of birth as recorded on your official identity documents. Use the Australian date format (day/month/year). Do not use the US format (month/day/year) as this will cause errors in your application.

⚠️ Common Mistakes

  • Using MM/DD/YYYY format instead of DD/MM/YYYY
  • Date differs from passport — DHA cross-checks this field
  • Entering a date for a different family member
